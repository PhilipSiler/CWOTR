 New switch WOTR_LAMBDA=<l> (TD-Gammon style target: (1 - l) x the same side's estimate at its next decision + l x that decision's target). td1 (next estimate = the score of the move chosen there) and td2 (next estimate = the network's plain reading of the next position), l 0.7 and 0.4, Shadow alone vs frozen fp_aw7: ALL collapse within 20 rounds to 6-13% wins. Void as a verdict: a side makes about 125 decisions a game, so with l per DECISION the result's weight vanishes after a few decisions and the network learns almost only from its own neighbouring estimates. To retry with l near 0.98 (a horizon of several turns).

 BOT MODE built (proto/bridge.c, "bridge bot <player>"): our player as a wotr-protocol bot on stdin/stdout; it only answers with one of the referee's options. How it answers: at an ACTION question the position is rebuilt from the view (hidden cards dealt at random) and our player chooses with its own engine; its own follow-up questions (next Army, recruit, Companions, Nazgul - the whole-plan Nazgul move is carried out one figure at a time -, a card's questions) come from the position it then holds; battle questions by starting the same attack on the view's board and re-rolling until the question comes up as described (a casualty with that many hits; the others after a round without losses); the Hunt by replaying our own Fellowship move until the damage matches; the Fellowship phase and Hunt allocation by ending the previous turn in our engine on the view's board; discards by the network's static value. A few written rules remain (advance, and whatever cannot be reached).
 Tested against our own referee with the same player run directly (cwotr1 = fp_aw4 + shadow_hunter1): 0 illegal answers in 600+ games; the rebuilt position lists the same ACTION options as the referee in 99.2%; new test WOTR_AGREE=1 (referee) compares every bot answer with the direct player's choice in the true position: action 76.9% vs 76.9% for the direct player against itself (Free Peoples), 61.5 vs 61.4 (Shadow), battle cards 53.5/54.4 and 48.3/45.8, Hunt allocation 82.8/84.8, entering Mordor 100/100; still behind: Hunt damage 75.0/82.5, Mithril 54.8/80.6, advance 63.6/88.6 (FP) and 91.2/94.6 (SH), casualties about 5 points.
